# Inviting Members

## Sending an invitation

1. Go to **Settings → Invites**
2. Enter the teammate's email address
3. Select their initial role (member or admin — you can change it later)
4. Click **Send Invite**

An invitation email is sent from `MCPHub <contact@mail.aniruddha.fyi>`. The invite link is valid for 7 days.

## Accepting an invitation

When the recipient clicks the link:
- If they already have an MCPHub account, they're added to the workspace immediately
- If they don't have an account, they're taken to a signup form — the invite is accepted automatically on signup

After accepting, the workspace appears in their workspace switcher.

## Pending invites

Pending invites are listed in **Settings → Invites**. Admins and owners can:
- See who was invited and when
- Revoke a pending invite before it's accepted

## Re-inviting

If an invite expires or was revoked, you can re-send to the same email. The old invite is automatically invalidated.

## Invite limits

There's no hard limit on the number of pending invites or workspace members.
